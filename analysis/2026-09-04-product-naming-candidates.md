# RumiAI — possible future product naming candidates

Status: **exploratory note — non-canonical**  
Date: 2026-09-04

## Current status

The current canonical product name remains **RumiAI**.

This note records a possible future naming reconsideration only. It does not rename the product, repositories, components, commands, namespaces, APIs, filesystem objects or any other established project object, and it does not establish aliases or migration requirements.

## Naming direction to preserve

In the future, RumiAI may change name.

One naming direction worth preserving is the concept:

**Artificial Person Intelligence**

The future name may be a direct acronym of this expression or a longer acronym/name that includes **Artificial Person Intelligence** as its semantic basis.

A second direction worth exploring is a name independent from the acronym itself but able to communicate the idea of an **artificial person**, rather than merely an assistant, chatbot or utility.

## Candidate names explicitly identified by the user

The following names are candidates for future evaluation and are **not approved product names**:

- **Aiumi**
- **Aiko**
- **Api** — **Artificial Person Intelligence**
- **Papi**
- **ApiOS**
- **Apia**
- another name or acronym that includes **Artificial Person Intelligence**
- a name independent from the acronym that can represent an artificial person rather than a simple assistant

### Aiumi — additional semantic association

**Aiumi** should also be evaluated for its resemblance in sound and form to the Italian word/phrase **“aiutami”** (“help me”).

This association may be useful because it makes the name immediately suggestive of support and interaction while still sounding like a personal name. It should nevertheless be evaluated carefully in a future naming phase because the “help me” association may also pull the perceived identity toward the narrower idea of an assistant, whereas the intended concept is broader: an artificial person.

## Additional exploratory human-style names proposed for evaluation

The following are assistant-proposed candidates only. They are recorded as brainstorming material and have no canonical or approved status.

- **Maia** — strongly human as a name, internationally pronounceable, and visually contains `AI` without looking like a technical acronym.
- **Ada** — concise and distinctly human; it also has a historical association with computing through Ada Lovelace, without describing the product as a tool or assistant.
- **Alma** — a human name with associations to “soul” or inner identity in several cultural contexts; potentially relevant to the idea of a persistent artificial person, although that semantic weight would need careful evaluation.
- **Mira** — short, human and international; it can suggest attention, observation and presence without explicitly reducing the identity to assistance.
- **Lia** — very short and clearly name-like; in Italian typography it also ends in `IA`, creating a subtle connection to *intelligenza artificiale* without making that connection the whole name.
- **Mia** — a familiar human name that can also evoke something personal or “mine”, potentially fitting a personal cognitive system; the ordinary-word meaning and high commonness would need evaluation.
- **Nora** — recognizably human, neutral and easy to pronounce across several languages; it carries little technical baggage, which may help emphasize personhood rather than software.
- **Elia** — human, soft and internationally recognizable; it contains `IA` while remaining first and foremost a personal name.

These suggestions should eventually be assessed not only for sound and meaning but also for ambiguity, existing products and organizations, trademarks, domains, repository names, pronunciation across major languages and unwanted cultural associations before any naming decision is made.

## Decision status

No naming decision is made by this note.

Until an explicit future decision supersedes it, **RumiAI** remains the authoritative product name and all existing canonical repository/component names remain unchanged.
